## Generated for this theme

The twelve marked Gemini were made with Google Gemini from prompts written
against the ENCOM palette, then selected, named and recompressed here.

## From other Omarchy themes

Matched byte for byte against the installed originals, so the source is
established rather than remembered.

| File | Source | Author |
|---|---|---|
| `07-crt-late-night.webp` | [omarchy-commit-theme](https://github.com/c0ze/omarchy-commit-theme) | c0ze |

Neither repository ships a licence file, so these are used with attribution and
without an explicit grant. If either author would rather they were not
redistributed here, open an issue and they come out.

## From Wallhaven

`06-void.png` was downloaded from Wallhaven as `173801.png`. The wallpaper page
for that id no longer resolves, so the uploader could not be identified. It is a
near-black gradient — 72 unique colours across the frame. Kept with its origin
stated as far as it can be established; happy to remove it on request.

## Third-party marks

The ENCOM International wordmark composed into
`assets/solaros-1984-phosphor-master.svg` is a third-party vector of a logo from
*Tron*. ENCOM is a fictional corporation; this theme is an unaffiliated fan work
and claims no rights over the mark.
